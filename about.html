About RE-SAIKEL

Building a smarter and more sustainable recycling ecosystem through technology.

Our Mission

RE-SAIKEL was founded to solve a fundamental problem: recyclable materials are wasted every day because the connection between households, businesses, and recyclers is broken. We fix that with technology — making recycling as easy as ordering a ride.

Our Vision

We envision a circular economy where no recyclable material ends up in landfill. By 2030, RE-SAIKEL aims to serve 1 million active users across Indonesia, diverting 500,000 tonnes of waste annually from landfills and incinerators.

Our Values

We believe in radical transparency, community-first design, and technology as a force for environmental good. Every feature we build is measured by its real-world impact on waste diversion and community empowerment.

Our Story

Started in a small Jakarta co-working space in 2022, RE-SAIKEL grew from frustration with the broken informal recycling chain. Three years later, we've connected over 12,000 households with 320+ certified recycling partners nationwide.
