Frequently Asked Questions

How long does it take for a partner to arrive?
Most partners arrive within your selected 1-hour window. You will receive an SMS with live GPS tracking once your booking is confirmed.
Can I mix different types of waste in one pickup?
Yes. Select the primary category, and mention any additional waste types in your notes. Partners are equipped to handle mixed loads.
How do Green Points work?
You earn points for every completed pickup and marketplace purchase. Points are credited to your wallet immediately and can be redeemed for rewards in your Profile page.
Can I reschedule or cancel a booking?
Yes. Bookings can be rescheduled up to 2 hours before the pickup window by contacting your assigned partner directly via the tracking screen.
Is my location data stored?
Your location is shared only with your assigned partner and is removed from their device after pickup is confirmed. We do not sell location data.
