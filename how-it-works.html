How It Works

From your doorstep to circular economy impact — in four clear steps.

1
📷

Upload Waste Information

Take a photo or manually select your waste type using our smart classifier. The platform identifies the category, estimates weight, and matches your pickup zone to available recyclers in your area.

2
📅

Schedule a Pickup

Choose a convenient date and time from our interactive calendar. A certified recycling partner near you is automatically assigned and you'll receive an SMS confirmation with live GPS tracking.

3
🏭

Processing & Sorting

Collected materials are weighed, sorted by category, and processed at certified partner facilities using eco-friendly methods. You receive a weight confirmation and green points are credited to your account.

4
🛒

Resell & Reuse

Recycled and processed materials enter the RE-SAIKEL marketplace. Buyers and manufacturers purchase directly. You track your cumulative environmental impact on your personal dashboard.
